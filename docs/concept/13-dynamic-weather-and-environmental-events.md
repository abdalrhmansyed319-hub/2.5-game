## 1. Core Weather Philosophy: Weather Is a Gameplay Partner, Not a Particle Filter
Too many games slap semi-transparent rain streaks over the camera and call it "dynamic weather." On Oura-IV, **every weather event physically alters sightlines, traversal routes, shelter psychology, organism behavior, and rare scientific opportunities!**
