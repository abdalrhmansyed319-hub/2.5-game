# Deliverable Y — Vertical Slice Acceptance Criteria (*"The Rimlands Awakening"*)

> **Purpose**: Define the **exact, testable pre-production contract** for *VERDANT SIGNAL*'s first playable milestone (**8–12 weeks**, preceded by a **1–2 week Windows Visual & Streaming Prototype Gate**).  
> **North Star**: The Vertical Slice does NOT exist to tick feature checkboxes — it exists to **prove *VERDANT SIGNAL*'s unmistakable soul**: *feeling gloriously small inside a living painted alien coastline, learning from autonomous wildlife, surviving a storm by reading nature, solving one real scientific hypothesis, and feeling an unforgettable chill of planetary mystery.*

---

## 1. Playable Footprint & Locked Content Roster (Zero Contradictions!)

- **Map Footprint**: **`4 × 4` Chunks (`16` `64×64`-tile chunks = `4096×4096 px`, ~25–40 minutes of unhurried play)** built via author-composed macro layouts + offline ecological scatter assist + feathered landmark blend collars, streamed live through a `3×3` active chunk window.
- **Included Environments (`2` Core Biomes + `1` Teaser Ecotone Border)**:
  1. **Amber Kelp Littoral**: *Drop-Pod Clearing* (Hero Landmark #1 — Home Camp) & *Drift-Wreck Cove* (Hero Landmark #2 — Tidal Salvage Basin).
  2. **Singing Reedwood Canopy**: *Hollow Flute Grove* (Hero Landmark #3 — Relay Node 1 Site) & *Sun-Dappled Glades*.
  3. **Vitreous Fen Ecotone Border** *(Teaser Edge)*: Visible emerald Acid Mist barrier blocking deeper basin entry — proving why scientific synthesis unlocks new horizons!
- **Locked Vertical Slice Organism Roster (`6` Flora + `6` Fauna = `12` Bespoke Organisms)**:
  - **6 Flora**: `Amber Tide-Kelp` (`fl_01`), `Mirror Glasswort` (`fl_02`), `Singing Reedwood` (`fl_03`), `Sun-Drop Bulb` (`fl_04`), `Cordage Frond` (`fl_05`), `Breath-Sponge Lichen` (`fl_06` — growing safely along the wet Canopy/Fen ecotone!).
  - **6 Fauna**: `Stilt-Strider` (`fa_01` — shy coastal flock), `Mirror-Shell Crab` (`fa_02` — tidal reflector/burrower), `Chime-Wing Glider` (`fa_03` — curious scanner-mimic!), `Prism-Pollen Moth` (`fa_04` — gentle pollinator swarm), `Lichen-Back Newt` (`fa_06` — peaceful Fen-border symbiote breathing cleanly inside emerald mist!), `Reed-Fang Ambush Lurker` (`fa_07` — single territorial tidal/marsh ambush predator testing stealth, Acoustic Decoy & Kinetic Core-Spike!).
- **Dynamic World Conditions**: Full LUT **Dawn -> Sun -> Dusk -> Bioluminescent Night** cycle (with accelerated `10-min` playtest mode + real `24-min` default) · **1 Twin-Moon Syzygy Low Tide** exposing *Drift-Wreck Cove* · **1 Rimlands Ion Tempest Squall** (`wx_02`) telegraphed `60 seconds` early by howling *Singing Reedwoods*!

## 2. What the Player Can Do (Verified Controls & Gameplay Verbs)

- **Locomotion & Feel**: Smooth 8-way Oblique 3/4 movement, **Crouch / Quiet Step** (visible reduced footstep noise ring), **Sprint / Short Dodge-Step** (uses Exertion Stamina), **Ledge-Hop / Low-Shelf Mantle**, smooth rounded collision sliding around trees/rocks (zero pixel-snagging), and full **Mouse+Keyboard + Gamepad parity**.
- **Field Science Verbs**:
  - Toggle **Acoustic Field Lens** (visualizes sound waves, Reedwood storm harmonics, creature alertness, and subtle hidden-tide acoustic echoes).
  - Frame & capture **Tier-1 Sighting Sketches** (`0.8s` responsive snap into illustrated Field Codex).
  - Gather **Tier-2 Samples & Behavioral Observations** (non-destructive clippings, acoustic chord capture, live observation of *Lichen-Back Newt* grazing on *Breath-Sponge*).
  - Use the **Camp Field Lab Open-Question Synthesis Board** to solve **2 concrete hypotheses** (*1: Acid-Mist Bio-Membrane Filter Mk1*, *2: Harmonic Relay Dish Stabilization*).
- **Survival & Field Defense Verbs**:
  - Pitch/use **Drop-Pod Camp** to rest (optional time-advance), brew analyzed botanical rations (`Amber Kelp Gel`, `Sun-Drop Tea`), and restore stamina/afflictions.
  - Shelter beneath **Singing Reedwood Arches** or Camp Tent during the Ion Tempest (watching rain/sparks bounce cleanly off overhead cover!).
  - Evade, distract, or fend off the *Reed-Fang Lurker* via **Crouch Stealth**, **Harmonic Decoy Chime**, or desperate **Kinetic Core-Spike** knockback!

## 3. What the Player Must Discover (Mystery-Preserving Act 1 Arc — NO Early Spoiler!)

To honor `DDL-002` (preserving the 4-layer planetary mystery!), the Vertical Slice MUST execute this exact discovery sequence:
1. **Immediate Orientation**: Wake beside smoking *ICV Meridian* Drop-Pod -> test Field Lens on coastal kelp/striders -> spot the shattered **Transmitter Dish** half-submerged in *Drift-Wreck Cove* (unreachable at high tide!).
2. **Living-World Lesson #1 (Wildlife Curiosity)**: Follow a curious **Chime-Wing Glider** mimicking Mara's scanner ping into *Singing Reedwood Canopy* -> discover natural fruit and the sweeping acoustic amphitheater of *Hollow Flute Grove*.
3. **Living-World Lesson #2 (Nature Warns You)**: Hear the Reedwoods' serene major wind-chords warp into urgent minor howling `60s` before an **Ion Tempest** -> survive the storm by reading nature and taking cover!
4. **Living-World Lesson #3 (Tidal World)**: Watch moons *Sela* & *Vesh* align on water reflections -> wade into drained *Drift-Wreck Cove* during **Twin-Moon Low Tide** (bypassing or luring a territorial *Reed-Fang Lurker*!) to recover the Transmitter Dish!
5. **Living-World Lesson #4 (Science IS Progression)**: Hit the emerald Acid Mist blocking the Fen edge -> watch a **Lichen-Back Newt** eating **Breath-Sponge Lichen** inside clean glowing air bubbles -> synthesize **Bio-Membrane Filter Mk1** on the Camp Synthesis Board!
6. **The Ambiguous Act 1 Cliffhanger (NOT a "Living Planet" Reveal!)**: Mount and harmonically tune **Relay Node 1** in *Hollow Flute Grove* -> orbital uplink fails against ionospheric static, **YET** the dish locks onto an eerie, repeating **prime-interval harmonic echo** bouncing straight out of the deep coastal basin and highland rock strata — **leaving Mara (and the player!) genuinely debating**: *Is it weird piezoelectric geology? A buried alien machine? Or another human distress beacon from a ship the Consortium never mentioned?* -> **End of Vertical Slice!**

## 4. Memorable Environment & Visual Quality Bar

- **Visual Prototype Gate (Completed BEFORE Full Vertical Slice Art)**:
  - Side-by-side Windows build verifying **`480×270` vs `640×360`** integer/sharp-bilinear scaling on real `1080p` & `1440p` monitors, smooth camera scrolling without shimmer, and `16×24` astronaut silhouette/tool readability under tall canopy fadeaways!
- **Mandatory "Living Painting" Standards**:
  - **Zero Visible Tile-Grid Monotony**: Curved organic shorelines, hanging grass tufts, grounded contact shadows under every tree/actor, and invisible feathered collars around all 3 Hero Landmarks.
  - **5-Band Contrast Enforcement**: Every screen passes desaturated grayscale squint inspection (`Actor/BG >= 4.5:1` per `tools/palette_director.py`) across Dawn, Sun, Dusk, Night, and Storm LUT states!
  - **Breathing Motion**: Pixel-snapped wind-sway shader on reeds/kelp, animated shoreline foam + sky/moon water reflection, drifting golden pollen & raindrop ground splashes, and translucent overhead canopy cutaways when Mara walks behind tall trees.
  - **Audio Atmosphere**: Surface-aware footsteps (`sand`, `shallow_water`, `reedwood_floor`, `moss`), non-repeating `AudioStreamRandomizer`, spatialized wildlife calls, dynamic 60s Reedwood storm chords, and 40–50% musical silence ratio!

## 5. Ecological Interactions That MUST Work Autonomously (Live Without Scripted Cutscenes!)

1. **Trophic Grazing**: *Stilt-Striders* autonomously move to *Amber Tide-Kelp* patches to feed and scatter when Mara sprints loudly (staying calm if Mara crouches!).
2. **Inter-Species Predation / Avoidance**: *Reed-Fang Lurker* stalks loud splashes or passing *Stilt-Striders* at dusk/fog, and reliably pivots to investigate a tossed **Harmonic Decoy Chime**.
3. **Pollinator / Mimic Curiosity**: *Chime-Wing Gliders* echo Mara's Field Lens chime and flutter toward blooming *Sun-Drop Bulbs* (which visibly close petals at night!).
4. **Weather Reactivity**: Before/during the Ion Tempest, *Stilt-Striders* huddle behind coastal boulders, *Mirror-Shell Crabs* burrow during tidal surge, and *Singing Reedwoods* shift audio + sway intensity!
5. **Symbiotic Science Clue**: At the Fen ecotone border, a *Lichen-Back Newt* autonomously grazes *Breath-Sponge Lichen* and vents a visible clean-air pocket inside low emerald mist — giving the player an unmistakable, text-free scientific "aha!" moment!

## 7. Blind Human Playtest Success Criteria (`L6` Kill/Go Greenlight Gate)

Before expanding production to Acts 2–4, **at least `5` blind external playtesters** (given zero verbal hints!) must evaluate the Vertical Slice against these measurable thresholds:
- **Aesthetic Hook (`>= 4 / 5` testers)**: Unprompted positive response to visual atmosphere (*"painterly / atmospheric / living world"*) and zero complaints about losing sight of Mara in foliage or storms.
- **Diegetic Navigation (`>= 4 / 5` testers)**: Locate *Drift-Wreck Cove* and *Hollow Flute Grove* using landmarks, sightlines, and audio/lens cues without needing quest arrows.
- **Scientific "Aha!" (`>= 4 / 5` testers)**: Independently connect the *Lichen-Back Newt* / *Breath-Sponge* observation to crafting the *Bio-Membrane Filter* on the Synthesis Board without trial-and-error frustration!
- **Autonomous World Feel (`>= 4 / 5` testers)**: Report witnessing at least **one unscripted wildlife/weather moment** that made Oura-IV feel genuinely alive outside their control.
- **Mystery Pull (`>= 4 / 5` testers)**: Express genuine curiosity after the Relay Node 1 cliffhanger and articulate at least one personal theory about what the signal is (*wanting to keep playing!*).

## 8. Deliberate Exclusions (Strictly OUT of Scope for the Vertical Slice!)

To protect schedule, art polish, and focus, the following systems are **explicitly banned** from *The Rimlands Awakening*:
- **NO** remaining 10 biomes (*Vitreous Fen interior*, *Fungal Jungle*, *Basalt Terraces*, *Desert*, *Archipelago*, *Lagoon Reef*, *Peaks*, *Glacier*, *Caverns*, *Caldera*).
- **NO** general-purpose runtime procedural continent generator (author-composed chunk layouts + offline scatter assist only!).
- **NO** Buoyancy Skiff, underwater/intertidal lagoon vehicle physics, Tranquilizer Bio-Dart, Whisper-Moss Stealth Boots, or multi-region fast-travel.
- **NO** full *Surveyor-01* wreck interior, Kaelen Voss caches, Keystone Leviathans, or Endings A/B/C cinematics.
- **NO** home-base terrarium farming, modular building construction, or localization beyond English strings (though string-key hygiene is enforced!).
- **NO** procedural multi-biome Ecotype variant swapping (every organism in the Vertical Slice is its singular, hand-crafted core species!).
