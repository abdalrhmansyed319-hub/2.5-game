# Deliverable J — Scientific Exploration and Discovery System

> **Core Innovation**: Inspired by *Outer Wilds*'s tactile translator tool and *In Other Waters*'s xenobiological intimacy — **Science in *VERDANT SIGNAL* is NOT "hold E for 3 seconds to fill a boring progress bar."** It is a tactile, 3-tier **Field Observation -> Ecological Experiment -> Synthesis Board** game loop that rewards genuine curiosity!

---

## 1. The 3-Tier Scientific Mastery System (Per Organism & Phenomenon)

Every flora species, fauna species, mineral, and weather event progresses through **3 distinct, rewarding discovery tiers** logged in Mara's illustrated **Field Codex**:

| Discovery Tier | How the Player Earns It | Immediate Gameplay Reward |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier 1 — Sighting & Sketch** | Frame the organism cleanly through the **Field Lens** (quick photographic snap/sketch — takes `0.8s`, never freezes movement awkwardly!). | Logs common silhouette, biome habitat, basic edibility/danger warning icon on world HUD, and adds a painterly hand-drawn pixel sketch to the Field Codex! |
| **Tier 2 — Live Behavioral / Sample Study** | **Active Field Science**: Collect a physical clipping/mineral core, record its **Acoustic Signature** during a storm/night, OR stealthily observe an **autonomous ecological interaction** (e.g., watching a *Lichen-Newt* eat *Breath-Sponge*, or seeing a *Storm-Fern* ground lightning!). | Unlocks full chemical/acoustic traits, reveals predator-prey relationships on the regional Codex web, and adds safe culinary/remedy recipes (no more risky blind tasting!). |
| **Tier 3 — Lab Hypothesis Synthesis** | Combine **2–3 Tier-2 Field Samples / Acoustic Recordings** on the **Camp Field Lab Synthesis Board** to solve a concrete scientific hypothesis! | **Unlocks permanent new Suit Adaptations, Ecological Tools, Bio-Remedies, and Planetary Relay Blueprints!** |

---

## 2. The Camp Field Lab Open-Question Synthesis Board (Anti-Frustration "Aha!" Progression)

> **Revision Note (Pre-Production Lock — see [`DDL-005`](24-design-decision-log.md))**: To prevent frustrating "guess which 2 of 40 icons click together" moon-logic puzzles, the Synthesis Board is structured around **Open Field Questions** automatically logged when Mara encounters an environmental barrier or ecological mystery!

- **How It Plays Cleanly Without Spoiling Discovery**:
  1. Hitting an obstacle (e.g., Acid Mist at the Fen edge) automatically pins an **Open Question Card** (*"How does local life survive Vitreous Acid Mist?"*) with **2 typed evidence slots** (e.g., `[Botanical Filter Sample]` + `[Observed Biological Adaptation]`).
  2. Only compatible evidence tags snap cleanly into a slot, and Mara's handwritten Field Note gives a subtle ecological nudge (*"Check what the marsh amphibians graze near the mist border"*), guaranteeing genuine detective satisfaction with zero blind icon mashing!

- **Example Hypothesis 1 — *Surviving Vitreous Acid Mist***:
  - *Clue Observed in Field*: Along the safe wet *Singing Canopy / Fen Ecotone*, Mara watches `Lichen-Back Newts` breathing cleanly inside emerald mist by grazing `Breath-Sponge Lichen`.
  - *Synthesis Pair*: `[Sample: Breath-Sponge Lichen]` + `[Behavioral Note: Newt Respiration]` -> **Synthesizes `Bio-Membrane Suit Filter Mk1`!**
- **Example Hypothesis 2 — *Surviving Quartz Peaks Lightning***:
  - *Clue Observed in Field*: Lightning strikes a ridge cleanly into a `Grounding Storm-Fern` while nearby rocks shatter.
  - *Synthesis Pair*: `[Sample: Grounding Storm-Fern]` + `[Sample: Verdigris Copper Salts]` -> **Synthesizes `Arc-Grounded Suit Lining` + `Relay Surge Capacitor`!**
- **Example Hypothesis 3 — *The Endgame Bio-Remediation Cure***:
  - *Clue Observed in Field*: Only `Cinder-Bloom Mat` survives beside the toxic *Surveyor-01* husk, while `Aurora Ice-Lotus` stabilizes volatile thermal reactions and `Choir Root-Nerve` distributes nutrients continent-wide!
  - *Synthesis Triad*: `[Cinder-Bloom]` + `[Aurora Ice-Lotus]` + `[Choir Root-Nerve]` -> **Synthesizes `Living Choir Remediation Culture`** (unlocking Endings B & C!).

## 3. Acoustic Field Lens — Seeing the Invisible World
Because Oura-IV's mystery is fundamentally **bio-acoustic**, Mara's Field Lens has a toggleable **Harmonic Spectrum Overlay** (subtle painterly wave-ripples, never cluttered Ubisoft detective-vision vomit!):
- Visualizes **invisible sound rings** (footstep noise radius, distant predator stalks, underground *Sonar Bell-Wing* cave echoes outlining secret rock fissures!).
- Displays **Harmonic Frequency Waveforms** on Singing Reedwoods, Migrating Horn-Beasts, Piezoelectric Quartz Spires, and subterranean Root-Nerves — turning **relay tuning** into a joyful, tactile musical/acoustic matching puzzle!
- Captures **Field Photographs / Sketches** saved directly to Mara's illustrated Journal pages, encouraging players to document breathtaking vistas and rare wildlife moments purely for joy!
