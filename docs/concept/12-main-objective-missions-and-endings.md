## 1. Critiquing & Elevating the Original "Build a Signal Tower" Objective

Why does *"collect 100 metal + 50 wires -> build one radio tower -> get rescued"* feel flat? Because it reduces a vast mysterious planet into a shopping list!

**Our Reinvention — *The Planetary Harmonic Relay Array*:**
Oura-IV's storm-charged ionosphere blocks ordinary narrowband radio signals completely! To reach orbit without brute-forcing a toxic nuclear reactor, Mara must **weave her salvaged *ICV Meridian* transceiver into Oura-IV's continental resonance strata** across **Three Interlocking Harmonic Nodes + Solving the Caldera Core**:

| Objective Pillar | Location | Why It Is Difficult & Ecologically Meaningful |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Node 1 — The Acoustic Anchor** *(Act 1 -> Act 2)* | **Singing Reedwood Canopy** *(Hollow Flute Groves)* | Salvage *Meridian* transmitter dish from *Drift-Wreck Cove* during **Twin-Moon Low Tide**; align struts with Singing Reedwoods & tune harmonics. *Picks up an eerie prime-interval subterranean echo — igniting the 4-Hypothesis mystery without giving away the answer!* |
| **Node 2 — The Subterranean Conduit** *(Act 2 -> Act 3)* | **Deep Mycelial Caverns** *(Root-Nerve Nexus)* | Cross Acid Mist & Spore-Fall to reach the subterranean Choir roots; earn peaceful passage from the **Choir Root-Weavers** using the Harmonic Greeting (never brute-force combat!) to tap a non-invasive mycelial bio-cable without harming the root! *Exposes the planetary intelligence & creeping grey necrosis tracing back to the Caldera!* |
| **Node 3 — The Piezoelectric Spire** *(Act 3)* | **Resonant Quartz Peaks** *(Piezoelectric Spires)* | Brave alpine lightning storms (sheltering beside Grounding Storm-Ferns) and assemble a copper-wound quartz resonator tower—only to discover **static interference from the leaking *Surveyor-01* catalyst in the Caldera corrupts the orbital frequency!** |
| **Final Convergence — The Caldera Choice** *(Act 4)* | **The Blighted Caldera** *(Surveyor-01 Wreck Core)* | Traverse radiation valleys in the moving steam-shadow of the ancient **Cinder-Back Tortoise** to reach *Surveyor-01*'s core. Here, Mara's accumulated ecological conduct, forensic discoveries, and physical intervention converge! |

## 2. Optional Exploration Activities & Emergent World Stories (Part 3L)
Beyond the 4-pillar main spine, Oura-IV rewards unguided wandering with rich, non-checklist discoveries:
- **7 Hidden *Surveyor-01* Caches (Kaelen Voss's Trail)**: Optional environmental detective arc scattered across underwater shoals, desert fossils, and glacier crevices—unlocking Ending C's philosophical realization.
- **4 Keystone Communion Encounters**: Non-violent acoustic duets with the planet's four rarest giants (*Horn-Beast Matriarch*, *Tide-Singing Ray*, *Root-Weaver Elder*, *Cinder-Back Tortoise*)—permanently expanding Mara's Field Harmonics and revealing hidden shortcuts!
- **Tidal & Weather-Gated Micro-Ecosystems**: Secret caverns accessible ONLY during Twin-Moon Low Tide; glowing fungal amphitheaters blooming ONLY during a Blooming Tide; Aurora Ice-Lotuses opening ONLY inside Polar Whiteouts!
- **Dynamic Emergent Moments**: Stumbling onto a Rift-Hound pack retreating from a circling Horn-Beast herd; rescuing a trapped Chime-Glider from a Mimic Pitcher and having its flock guide you to a hidden cargo pod!

## 3. Three Distinct Endings (Earned From Accumulated World State — See `Deliverable D` & `DDL-003`)
1. **Ending A — *Extraction (The Cold Signal)***: Rip *Surveyor-01*'s intact fusion core out and bolt it into the Quartz Peaks spire to brute-force a distress beam right through the interference *(available earliest — or default path if Mara damaged the Root-Nerves and skipped healing/Keystones!)* -> Consortium extraction ship arrives; Oura-IV's Choir falls silent under corporate claim beacons.
2. **Ending B — *Symbiosis (The Living Chorus)***: Requires **(a)** healing the Caldera scar with *Bio-Remediation Culture*, **(b)** peaceful Ecological Conduct / >= 2 Keystone Harmonics so the Choir actively locks into harmony with your Relay Array during a **Blooming Tide**, and **(c)** recovering *Surveyor-01*'s black-box bypass to broadcast openly to *all* Earth scientific receivers -> Rescue arrives cleanly alongside international biosphere-protection mandates.
3. **Ending C — *The Witness (Quiet Horizon)***: Requires **(a)** healing the Caldera scar AND **(b)** recovering all 7 of Kaelen Voss's buried field logs -> Instead of triggering the Quartz Peaks broadcast, Mara physically carries the salvaged corporate transponder to the **Glass-Water Kelp Drop** and sinks it permanently into the abyss, shielding Oura-IV from humanity's gaze forever as she stays behind to listen, learn, and belong.
