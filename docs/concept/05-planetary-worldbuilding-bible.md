# Deliverable E — Planetary Worldbuilding Bible (*Oura-IV / Catalog K-419b*)

> **Worldbuilding Philosophy**: Grounded astrobiology + poetic visual spectacle. Every biome, river, storm, mineral vein, and alien organism on **Oura-IV** obeys one interlocking geophysical engine so the planet feels astonishingly alien *and* scientifically inevitable.

---

## 1. Planetary Astrophysics & Physical Baseline

| Parameter | Oura-IV Specification | Direct Gameplay & Visual Consequence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parent Star & Orbit** | Warm K-type orange-gold dwarf star (*Aurelia-K*); 340-day gentle orbital year | Rich golden-amber daylight bias; lush teal/violet/ochre photosynthetic pigments (NOT generic Earth neon-green!) |
| **Twin Moons** | ***Sela*** (large, pale pearl, close orbit) & ***Vesh*** (small, rust-iron outer moon) | Drives dramatic **Twin-Moon Syzygy Tides** exposing sunken ruins/reefs every 3 cycles; visible reflected in calm littoral water! |
| **Surface Gravity** | `0.86 g` (slightly lighter than Earth) | Justifies towering cathedral-scale umbrella-fungi, slender stilt-legged striders, sweeping soaring kites, and crisp agile traversal |
| **Day / Night Cycle** | `24 real-time minutes` = 1 Oura-IV Rotation (`Dawn Mist 3m`, `High Sun 10m`, `Golden Dusk 3m`, `Bioluminescent Night 8m`) | Long enough to plan expeditions without frantic rushing; short enough that every play session witnesses dramatic dawn fog, golden twilight, and glowing night ecology! |
| **Atmosphere** | `74% N2, 22% O2, 1.8% Ar, 2.2% trace sulfur/halogen/mycelial aerosols` | **Breathable in clean temperate lowlands** (no annoying oxygen bar at home camp!), yet volatile trace particulates concentrate into lethal **Acid Mist**, **Neuro-Spore Clouds**, and **High-Altitude Thin Air** in extreme biomes! |
| **Planetary Magnetism & Geology** | Unusually quartz-rich crust with dense **piezoelectric silicate veins** + strong tilted magnetic field | Powers Oura-IV's signature phenomenon: continental tectonic pressure + living mycelial filaments convert wind/tide/tectonic stress into **bio-electric harmonic pulses** (*The Blooming Tide*) and Polar Auroras! |

## 2. Continental Geography & Hydrological Engine

Oura-IV's playable hemisphere is organized as a **single coherent coastal-to-polar Continental Mosaic (6 Macro-Regions)** shaped by prevailing **West-to-East ocean winds** hitting the central **Highland Divide**:

```text
                       [6. POLAR CROWN: Rime-Glass Tundra & Glacier]
                                      ▲           ▲
                                      │           │
  [4. AZURE SEA]                      │     [3. HIGHLAND DIVIDE]
  Pelagic Archipelago ◄───────────────┴────► Resonant Quartz Peaks (Relay Site!)
        ▲                                           ▲
        │                                           │
  Bioluminescent Shelf ◄──► [1. SUNKEN RIMLANDS] ◄──► Steppe of Wind-Carved Basalt
                            Amber Kelp Littoral (CRASH)        │
                            Singing Reedwood Canopy            ▼
                                      │              [5. SUN-SCOURED RIFT]
                                      ▼              Ochre Salt-Canyon Desert
                            [2. LOWLAND BASIN]                 │
                            Vitreous Fen & Bog                 ▼
                            Vesper Fungal Jungle     The Blighted Caldera (CLIMAX)
                                      │                        ▲
                                      ▼                        │
                       [SUBTERRANEAN CHOIR: Deep Mycelial & Crystal Caverns]
                                 (Links ALL regions underground!)
```

- **Why Every Biome Exists Where It Does (Rain-Shadow & Elevation Causality)**:
  1. Warm moist ocean air sweeps eastward off the **Azure Sea**, soaking the **Sunken Rimlands** (`Amber Kelp Coast`, `Singing Canopy`) and pooling inside the sunken **Lowland Basin** (`Vitreous Fen`, `Vesper Fungal Jungle`).
  2. Rising air cools and discharges violent electrical precipitation against the **Highland Divide** (`Basalt Terraces` -> `Resonant Quartz Peaks` -> `Cryo-Glacier`).
  3. On the dry eastern leeward rain-shadow behind the mountains lies the ancient dried inland seabed of the **Sun-Scoured Rift** (`Ochre Salt-Canyon Desert` -> `The Blighted Caldera`).
  4. Directly beneath all continents winds the **Subterranean Choir** (`Deep Mycelial Caverns` + `Piezoelectric Geode Vaults`), serving as BOTH the planetary neural backbone AND a network of unlockable underground shortcuts!

## 3. Natural Energy Sources & Geological Stratigraphy

- **Piezoelectric Silicate Veins**: Stress-charged crystalline strata humming subtly underfoot; mined/tapped cleanly (not strip-mined) to power field relays.
- **Bio-Chemical Exothermic Mats & Hydrothermal Vents**: Localized warm oases in the Polar Glacier and Abyssal Shelf creating natural safe refuge islands inside hostile climates.
- **Three Visual Eras of Planetary History (Stratigraphy)**:
  1. *Deep Geological Age (Millions of Years)*: Hexagonal basalt columns, fossilized mega-root arcs in desert canyons, ancient calcified coral rings.
  2. *Living Choir Age (Current Native Ecology)*: Harmonious, vibrant symbiosis between piezoelectric geology, flora, and autonomous fauna.
  3. *Human Intrusion Scar (19 Years Ago & Today)*: Corroded weathered titanium wreckage of *Surveyor-01* (leaking grey dead catalyst in the Caldera) contrasted against Mara's fresh orange-white *ICV Meridian* crash debris in the Rimlands.

## 4. Seasonal Drift (Gentle Long-Cycle Planetary Shifts)
Rather than forcing players to wait 40 hours for seasons, Oura-IV exhibits **rhythmic micro-cycles** synced across multi-day expeditions:
- **Every 3 Days**: *Twin-Moon Syzygy Tide* (exposes sunken tidal shoals & lifts living kelp-raft bridges).
- **Every 5 Days**: *The Blooming Tide* (planet-wide bioluminescent resonance surge synchronizing root-nerves and temporarily scrambling unshielded electronics).
- **Regional Weather Fronts**: Dynamic storm cells travel visibly across the horizon from West to East (telegraphed 60 seconds early by **Singing Reedwoods** shifting from calm major chords into urgent minor howling!).
